# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on software together. Everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or elsewhere in the project's
spaces, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, not people;
- accept that others have different experience, background and views;
- refrain from harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks and unwelcome
  personal attention, and from publishing others' private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that do not follow this
code, and may exclude people who repeatedly or seriously disregard it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through GitHub
(@maltedreyer-5). Reports are treated confidentially.
